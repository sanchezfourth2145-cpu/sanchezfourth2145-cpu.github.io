These medals are not just trophies to display. They taught me discipline, teamwork, and how to get back up every time I lose. These experiences made me stronger, braver, and more ready to face any challenge that comes my way.

Baguio – Palarong Pambansa

It was such a big honor to represent our region in Palarong Pambansa held in Baguio. This experience taught me courage, sportsmanship, and how to carry our flag with pride. I met many new friends from different places, learned so many valuable lessons, and created memories that I will never forget for the rest of my life. This will always be one of my most unforgettable achievements.

SHS Graduate

Graduating from Senior High School at the University of the Cordillera is one of the most important milestones of my life. It marked the end of a challenging chapter and the beginning of new opportunities. I am proud of how far I’ve come, and I thank all the people who helped me reach this point. This achievement inspires me to keep chasing my dreams and work even harder for college.

Scholarship

I am proud to share that I got a full scholarship at the University of the Cordillera because of playing basketball. All the hard work, early practices, and sacrifices finally paid off. This scholarship gave me the chance to continue my education while doing what I love. It taught me that dedication, talent, and perseverance can open great opportunities for the future.
